# Travel Safety Guide

## General Safety Principles

1. **Research your destination** before travelling — check your government's official travel advisory.
2. **Register with your embassy** when travelling to higher-risk regions.
3. **Travel insurance** — Always purchase comprehensive travel insurance including medical evacuation.
4. **Copies of documents** — Keep digital copies of passport, visa, insurance, and itinerary in cloud storage.
5. **Emergency numbers** — Know the local emergency number (not always 112 or 911).

## Country Safety Ratings Overview

### Tier 1 — Exercise Normal Precautions
Countries generally considered safe for tourists with standard vigilance.
- Japan, Iceland, Switzerland, Singapore, New Zealand, Norway, Denmark, Finland, Austria, Portugal, Canada, Australia, Germany, Netherlands

### Tier 2 — Exercise Increased Caution
Higher rates of petty crime, occasional civil unrest, or specific regional risks.
- USA (petty crime in major cities), France (pickpocketing in tourist areas), Italy, Spain, Brazil (specific cities), Mexico (resort areas generally safe; northern border regions higher risk), South Africa (certain areas), Thailand, India, Morocco

### Tier 3 — Reconsider Travel
Significant safety concerns; travel with extra preparation or avoid non-essential travel.
- Parts of Central America, parts of Africa, certain Middle Eastern regions

### Tier 4 — Do Not Travel
Active conflict zones or extreme safety risks. Consult your government's travel advisory.

## Common Travel Scams

- **Taxi overcharging** — Always agree on a price before getting in, or insist on the meter.
- **Gem/carpet scam** — Stranger befriends you and leads you to an overpriced shop claiming you can resell items at home.
- **Fake police** — Request to see ID; real police do not ask tourists to hand over passports on the street.
- **Distraction theft** — Someone spills something on you while an accomplice steals your belongings.
- **ATM skimming** — Use ATMs inside bank branches; cover keypad when entering PIN.
- **Counterfeit tickets** — Buy attraction tickets only from official websites or on-site box offices.

## Health & Medical

- **Vaccinations** — Check CDC or WHO recommendations for your destination at least 6–8 weeks before departure.
- **Medications** — Carry prescription medications in original packaging with a doctor's letter.
- **Water safety** — Tap water is safe in Western Europe, North America, Australia, Japan. Use bottled water in many parts of Asia, Africa, and Latin America.
- **Food safety** — "Boil it, cook it, peel it, or forget it" in areas with food safety concerns.
- **Sun protection** — Use SPF 30+ sunscreen, especially in tropical or high-altitude destinations.
- **Altitude sickness** — Ascend gradually above 2,500m (8,200ft); consider acetazolamide for rapid ascent.

## Stolen Passport
1. Report to local police immediately and get a report number.
2. Contact your country's nearest embassy or consulate.
3. Have a digital copy accessible to expedite emergency passport issuance.
