# Frequently Asked Questions — Travel Planning

## General Planning

**Q: How far in advance should I book flights?**
A: For international flights, 2–6 months in advance generally gives the best prices. Domestic flights are often cheapest 1–3 months out. For peak seasons (Christmas, summer), book even earlier.

**Q: What is the best travel insurance?**
A: Look for policies that include: emergency medical evacuation (at least $100,000 coverage), trip cancellation/interruption, lost/delayed baggage, 24/7 emergency assistance line. World Nomads and Allianz are popular choices.

**Q: How much should I budget per day?**
A: Rough daily budgets (accommodation + food + local transport + 1 activity):
- **Budget travel**: $30–$60 (Southeast Asia, Eastern Europe), $50–$80 (Western Europe/Americas)
- **Mid-range**: $80–$150 per day globally
- **Luxury**: $200–$500+ per day

**Q: Is it safe to use public Wi-Fi while travelling?**
A: Avoid accessing banking or sensitive accounts on public Wi-Fi. Use a VPN for security. Purchase a local SIM card for reliable private mobile data.

## Visas & Entry

**Q: What is a visa on arrival (VOA)?**
A: A visa issued at the port of entry (airport or border crossing) rather than requiring advance application. Requirements (photos, forms, fees) vary by destination. Always carry required documents.

**Q: What is an eVisa?**
A: An electronic visa applied for and received online before travel, eliminating the need to visit a consulate. Many countries now offer eVisas, including India, Kenya, Australia, and others.

**Q: What documents do I need at immigration?**
A: Typically: valid passport (6 months beyond travel dates), return or onward ticket, proof of accommodation, sufficient funds evidence (bank statement or cash), and applicable visa if required.

## Money & Payments

**Q: Should I exchange currency before or after arriving?**
A: Airport exchange rates are typically poor. Use a local ATM on arrival for the best rates. Inform your bank before travelling to avoid fraud blocks. Charles Schwab and Wise cards offer fee-free international withdrawals.

**Q: Is it better to pay in local currency or home currency abroad?**
A: Always pay in LOCAL currency when given the choice. "Dynamic currency conversion" (DCC) to your home currency applies unfavourable exchange rates.

**Q: How much cash should I carry?**
A: Enough for 1–2 days of expenses as backup. In cash-heavy societies (Japan, parts of Southeast Asia), carry more. In card-friendly countries (Scandinavia, UK), minimal cash is needed.

## Packing

**Q: What is the ideal carry-on luggage size?**
A: Most airlines permit 55 x 40 x 20 cm (21.6 x 15.7 x 7.8 in) and up to 10 kg for carry-on. Always verify your specific airline's policy before packing.

**Q: What should I always pack?**
A: Must-haves:
- Photocopies/digital copies of all documents
- Universal travel adapter
- Portable charger/power bank
- Basic first-aid kit (pain reliever, antidiarrheal, band-aids)
- Reusable water bottle
- Neck pillow and eye mask for long flights

**Q: How do I avoid jet lag?**
A: Start shifting your sleep schedule 2–3 days before departure. Stay hydrated on the flight. Avoid alcohol and caffeine. On arrival, adjust immediately to local time and get sunlight exposure.

## Accommodation

**Q: What are the main types of accommodation?**
A: Hotels (most services, best for business/luxury), Hostels (budget, social, dorm or private rooms), Airbnb/Vrbo (home-like, good for families or long stays), Guesthouses/B&Bs (locally owned, personal service), Boutique hotels (unique, design-focused).

**Q: What should I check before booking accommodation?**
A: Location relative to attractions/transport, check-in/check-out flexibility, cancellation policy, recent reviews (aim for 4.0+ with 50+ reviews), included amenities (breakfast, Wi-Fi, parking).
