# Visa Requirements Guide

## How to Use This Guide
Visa requirements depend on your **passport nationality** and **destination country**.
Always verify current requirements with the official embassy or consulate — rules change frequently.

## Key Visa Concepts

**Visa-free / Visa on arrival** — No advance visa needed. Check permitted stay duration and entry requirements.
**eVisa** — Apply online before travel; approval typically within 3–5 business days.
**Embassy visa** — Must apply in person or by post at destination country's consulate; lead time 2–8 weeks.
**Visa run** — Exiting and re-entering a country to reset the permitted stay; increasingly restricted.

## USA Destinations

### US Citizens Travelling to Europe (Schengen Area)
- **Current**: Visa-free, up to 90 days in any 180-day period.
- **ETIAS (upcoming)**: Starting 2025, US citizens need to pre-register for an ETIAS (European Travel Information and Authorisation System) — low-cost online application, valid 3 years.
- **Schengen Zone**: Austria, Belgium, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland.

### US Citizens Travelling to the UK
- Visa-free for tourism and business, up to 6 months. Passport must be valid for the duration of stay.

### US Citizens Travelling to Japan
- Visa-free, up to 90 days for tourism.

### US Citizens Travelling to Australia
- eVisitor (subclass 651) — free, apply online at immi.homeaffairs.gov.au. Valid 12 months, multiple entries, up to 3 months per visit.

### US Citizens Travelling to India
- eVisa required. Apply at indianvisaonline.gov.in. Tourist eVisa valid 30 days to 5 years. Apply at least 4 days before travel.

## UK Passport Holders

### UK to EU / Schengen
- Visa-free, up to 90 days in any 180-day period. Full UK passport required (British National Overseas and other travel documents have different rules).

### UK to USA
- ESTA (Electronic System for Travel Authorization) required — apply at esta.cbp.dhs.gov. $21 fee, valid 2 years. Permits stays up to 90 days.

### UK to Australia
- eVisitor (651) — free, apply online. Up to 3 months per visit.

## Common Visa Pitfalls

1. **Passport validity** — Most countries require your passport to be valid for at least 6 months beyond your intended departure date.
2. **Blank pages** — Some countries (e.g. USA) require at least 2 blank pages in your passport.
3. **Proof of onward travel** — Many countries require a return or onward ticket at immigration.
4. **Dual nationality** — Some countries do not recognise dual nationality. Always enter using the passport of the destination country if you hold citizenship there.
5. **Yellow fever certificate** — Required for entry to certain African and South American countries if arriving from endemic regions.
6. **Criminal record** — USA, Australia, and Canada may deny entry to travellers with certain criminal records.
