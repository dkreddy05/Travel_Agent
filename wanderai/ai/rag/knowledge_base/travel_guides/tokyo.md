# Tokyo Travel Guide

Tokyo, Japan's sprawling capital, blends ultramodern technology with traditional culture, offering an unmatched travel experience.

## Top Attractions
- **Senso-ji Temple** — Tokyo's oldest temple in Asakusa. Best visited early morning before crowds.
- **Shibuya Crossing** — World's busiest pedestrian crossing; best viewed from Starbucks or Mag's Park above.
- **Shinjuku Gyoen** — Beautiful national garden; cherry blossom season (late March–early April) is spectacular.
- **teamLab Borderless** — Immersive digital art museum in Odaiba.
- **Tsukiji Outer Market** — Fresh sushi and street food in the morning.
- **Akihabara** — Electronics, anime, and gaming district.
- **Meiji Shrine** — Forested Shinto shrine dedicated to Emperor Meiji.

## Neighbourhoods
- **Shinjuku** — Neon-lit entertainment, nightlife, Golden Gai bars.
- **Shibuya** — Fashion, youth culture, major shopping.
- **Harajuku** — Trendy boutiques, Takeshita Street street fashion.
- **Yanaka** — Old Tokyo atmosphere, temples, artisan shops.
- **Roppongi** — Upscale nightlife, international restaurants, Mori Art Museum.

## Getting Around
- **IC Card (Suica/Pasmo)** — Reloadable card works on all trains, metro, and buses. Buy at any station.
- **JR Pass** — Only worthwhile if making multiple Shinkansen (bullet train) trips.
- **Taxi** — Expensive but widely available; flag fare starts around ¥500.
- Most tourist areas are walkable once you exit the station.

## Food & Drink
- **Ramen** — Regional styles: Sapporo (miso), Hakata (tonkotsu), Tokyo (shoyu).
- **Sushi** — Try kaiten (conveyor belt) sushi for budget eating; Tsukiji for premium.
- **Izakaya** — Japanese pub-style dining; great for sharing dishes with beer.
- **Convenience stores (7-Eleven, Lawson)** — Excellent onigiri, sandwiches, and hot food. A meal for ¥500–¥800.
- **Vending machines** — Hot and cold drinks everywhere; ¥120–¥160.

## Budget Guidance
- **Budget** (¥7,000–¥10,000/day): capsule hotel, convenience-store meals, free shrines.
- **Mid-range** (¥15,000–¥25,000/day): business hotel, restaurant meals, paid attractions.
- **Luxury** (¥50,000+/day): ryokan or 5-star hotel, omakase sushi, private experiences.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Spring (March–May)** — Cherry blossoms late March to mid-April; most popular time.
- **Autumn (October–November)** — Autumn foliage, comfortable temperatures.
- **Winter (December–February)** — Cold but dry; fewer tourists; illumination displays.
- **Summer (June–August)** — Hot and humid; typhoon season; festivals (matsuri).

## Practical Tips
- Carry cash — many small restaurants and shops don't accept cards.
- Google Maps works excellently for train navigation.
- Public behaviour etiquette: no eating while walking, speak quietly on trains.
- Most restrooms are free and very clean.
- English signage is common at major attractions and train stations.
