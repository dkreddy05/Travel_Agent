# New York City Travel Guide

New York City, the most populous city in the United States, is a global hub for finance, fashion, art, and culture.

## Top Attractions
- **Statue of Liberty & Ellis Island** — Book ferry tickets well in advance; half-day experience.
- **Central Park** — 843 acres of urban parkland. Free entry; great for running, picnics, and concerts.
- **Metropolitan Museum of Art (The Met)** — World-class collection spanning 5,000 years. Pay-what-you-wish for NY state residents.
- **Empire State Building** — Observation decks on 86th and 102nd floors.
- **Times Square** — Iconic neon hub; best experienced at night.
- **Brooklyn Bridge** — Free to walk; stunning skyline views from the pedestrian path.
- **High Line** — Elevated park on a former rail line; free entry.
- **MoMA** — Modern and contemporary art.

## Neighbourhoods
- **Manhattan Midtown** — Skyscrapers, Broadway theatres, Rockefeller Center.
- **Greenwich Village** — Bohemian history, NYU, jazz clubs.
- **Brooklyn: Williamsburg/DUMBO** — Hipster cafés, vintage shops, Manhattan Bridge views.
- **Harlem** — Jazz heritage, soul food, Apollo Theater.
- **Lower East Side** — Nightlife, street art, Jewish heritage.

## Getting Around
- **Subway** — 24/7 service, $2.90 per ride. OMNY contactless payment (tap card or phone).
- **Walking** — Midtown and downtown Manhattan very walkable; numbered grid system makes navigation easy.
- **Citibike** — Bike-share available across Manhattan and Brooklyn.
- **Taxi/Uber** — Readily available; traffic can be slow during peak hours.

## Food & Drink
- **Pizza** — New York-style thin crust; Joe's Pizza (Greenwich Village) is iconic.
- **Bagels** — Best from H&H Bagels or Ess-a-Bagel.
- **Deli sandwiches** — Katz's Delicatessen (Lower East Side) is legendary.
- **Diverse cuisine** — Chinatown, Little Italy, Flushing (Queens) for authentic ethnic food.
- **Food halls** — Chelsea Market, Urbanspace Vanderbilt offer upscale street food.
- **Happy hour** — Many bars offer discounted drinks 4–7 pm.

## Budget Guidance
- **Budget** ($80–$120/day): hostel dorm, street food, free attractions.
- **Mid-range** ($200–$350/day): 3-star hotel, restaurant meals, paid museums.
- **Luxury** ($500+/day): boutique or 5-star hotel, fine dining, premium experiences.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Spring (April–May)** — Mild weather, blooming trees, comfortable for walking.
- **Autumn (September–November)** — Crisp weather, fall foliage in Central Park.
- **Winter (December–February)** — Christmas atmosphere, ice skating; cold but manageable.
- **Summer (June–August)** — Hot and humid; many free outdoor events.

## Practical Tips
- Get a MetroCard or tap with contactless payment for the subway.
- NYC is a 24-hour city; most restaurants and services are open late.
- Tipping is expected: 18–20% at restaurants, $1–2 per drink at bars.
- Many world-class museums have free or pay-what-you-wish admission on certain days.
