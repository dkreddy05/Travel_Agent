# Paris Travel Guide

Paris, France is one of the world's most visited destinations, known for its art, culture, cuisine, and iconic landmarks.

## Top Attractions
- **Eiffel Tower** — The iconic iron lattice tower on the Champ de Mars. Tip: Book tickets online in advance to skip the queue. Evening illuminations run every hour.
- **The Louvre** — World's largest art museum. Home to the Mona Lisa and Venus de Milo. Closed Tuesdays.
- **Notre-Dame Cathedral** — Currently under restoration after the 2019 fire; exterior viewing available.
- **Musée d'Orsay** — Impressionist masterpieces in a converted railway station.
- **Palace of Versailles** — Day trip 30 min from Paris by RER C train.
- **Sacré-Cœur Basilica** — Hilltop basilica in Montmartre with panoramic city views.
- **Centre Pompidou** — Modern art museum with striking inside-out architecture.

## Neighbourhoods
- **Le Marais** — Historic Jewish quarter, boutiques, falafel, gay-friendly.
- **Saint-Germain-des-Prés** — Literary cafés, upscale shopping, art galleries.
- **Montmartre** — Bohemian hill village, artist studios, Moulin Rouge.
- **Latin Quarter** — Student area near Sorbonne, affordable bistros.
- **Champs-Élysées** — Famous avenue, luxury shopping, Arc de Triomphe.

## Getting Around
- **Metro** — 16 lines covering the entire city. Buy a carnet (10-trip book) for savings.
- **Vélib'** — City bike-share scheme, great for flat central areas.
- **Walking** — Central Paris is very walkable. Many attractions within 2 km of each other.

## Food & Drink
- Breakfast: croissant and café au lait at a local boulangerie.
- Lunch: try a croque-monsieur or salade niçoise at a brasserie.
- Dinner: prix-fixe menus (menu du jour) offer the best value, typically €15–€30.
- Iconic foods: macarons (Ladurée), baguettes, steak-frites, crêpes, escargot.
- Wine is cheaper at supermarkets (Monoprix) than restaurants.

## Budget Guidance
- **Budget** (€60–€90/day): free museums on first Sunday, picnics by the Seine, hostel.
- **Mid-range** (€120–€200/day): 3-star hotel, restaurant meals, main museums.
- **Luxury** (€300+/day): 5-star hotels (Ritz, Le Bristol), Michelin-starred dining.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Spring (April–June)** — Mild weather, blooming parks, fewer crowds than summer.
- **Summer (July–August)** — Peak tourist season; many Parisians leave in August.
- **Autumn (September–November)** — Pleasant weather, fewer tourists, fashion week.
- **Winter (December–February)** — Christmas markets, quieter museums, cold but manageable.

## Practical Tips
- Most museums offer free entry on the first Sunday of each month.
- Tipping is not mandatory; rounding up to the nearest euro is standard.
- Many restaurants close between 2 pm and 7 pm.
- Tap water (eau du robinet) is free and safe at all restaurants.
